The system

Four rules. One clean way to read any chart.

  1. 01Pushes and pullbacks
  2. 02Horizontal zones
  3. 03Daily closes decide
  4. 04Fractal alignment

Pushes and pullbacks

Price does two things: it pushes and it pulls back. Higher highs and higher lows mean up. Lower highs and lower lows mean down. A break of structure is the only thing that changes the story.

Horizontal zones

Never a single line. A zone is where past structure turned price, wide enough to hold the real decision. We map only the zones that mattered, and nothing else.

Daily closes decide

A spike through a zone proves nothing. A daily close beyond it confirms the break. We act on closes, never on noise.

Fractal alignment

Weekly and daily structure give the direction. The 1-hour structure gives the entry. When all three agree, you have a setup worth taking seriously.
