One cycle, three views

Every trend moves through the same loop.

The tool reads each stock at three stages. When you know the stage, you know what to watch for next.

  1. Stage 1

    Reversal

    Price returns to a weekly swing zone and a 4-hour close moves back out of it. The first sign that a trend may turn.

  2. Stage 2

    Momentum

    The trend has changed and price holds beyond its trend change point. These are the stocks already moving.

  3. Stage 3

    Retracement

    A pullback measured as a share of the last swing. The 45% to 65% zone is where it becomes interesting to watch.

Then the next Reversal begins, and the loop repeats
